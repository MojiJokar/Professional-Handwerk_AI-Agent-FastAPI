project-based course. The ultimate goal is to build a real application in VS Code that follows this workflow:

Email → AI Agent → Analysis → CRM → Calendar → Response → Human Approval → Send

Along the way, you will learn Python, FastAPI, APIs, databases, authentication, AI agents, tools, and eventually deployment.
